Ask the Quran

A free search over the Quran in English and Persian. You get verses and translations — never commentary, never a ruling.

It can only answer from Quranic text. If no relevant verse is found, it refuses.

How to read this

  • The Arabic is the Quran, verbatim. Nothing on this site changes it.
  • A translation is interpretation, not the Quran. Each translation is labelled with its translator.
  • No tafsir and no explanation are shown. For meaning, consult a qualified scholar.
  • Verse links go to quran.com and tanzil.net; the text here comes from Tanzil and QuranEnc.
